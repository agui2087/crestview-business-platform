# Legal-document status

The files in this directory are operational drafts, not legal advice and not approved public policies. They must be reviewed by qualified counsel for Crestview's actual legal entity, jurisdiction, business model, customer base, vendor contracts, insurance, and data practices before publication or pilot use.

Do not replace bracketed items by guessing. Record counsel approval, effective date, and the exact published version in the release record.

- `privacy-policy-draft.md`: external privacy-notice draft
- `terms-of-service-draft.md`: external product-terms draft
- `data-retention-and-deletion-policy.md`: internal control schedule and request procedure
- `subprocessor-register.md`: vendor disclosure/control register
- `counsel-review-checklist.md`: decisions requiring legal advice
