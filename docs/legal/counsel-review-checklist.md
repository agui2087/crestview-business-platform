# Counsel review checklist

- [ ] Confirm full Crestview legal entity, address, contacts, launch jurisdictions, user types, minimum age, and contracting model
- [ ] Determine controller/processor roles for buyers, brokers, organizations, workforce data, acquisition documents, and pilot research
- [ ] Approve lawful bases, consent/withdrawal, user-rights, authorized-agent, appeal, and response procedures
- [ ] Decide whether uploaded documents may include special-category, financial, employment, health, consumer-report, or regulated data; prohibit unsupported categories
- [ ] Review all vendor DPAs, subprocessors, regions, transfer mechanisms, deletion, security commitments, and breach terms
- [ ] Approve Cloudmersive file transfer before the production key is added
- [ ] Approve retention schedule, backup-expiry representation, legal holds, transaction records, NDA acceptance, billing/tax records, and deletion exceptions
- [ ] Ensure Stripe price, renewal, cancellation, tax, refund, and consumer auto-renewal language matches the live UI
- [ ] Draft warranty, liability, indemnity, dispute, sanctions/export, notice, and enterprise-order terms
- [ ] Approve electronic signature/NDA workflow and clarify Crestview does not draft or approve agreements
- [ ] Review marketing statements about security, malware scanning, AI, verification, brokers/listings, availability, backups, compliance, and “enterprise-grade” status
- [ ] Approve pilot consent, research recording, compensation, withdrawal, confidentiality, and participant-data handling
- [ ] Determine cookie/tracking notice requirements; pilot telemetry is first-party but still requires review
- [ ] Define incident/breach notification obligations and escalation contacts
- [ ] Approve final English version and obtain qualified Spanish translation/legal review before publishing Spanish legal text
