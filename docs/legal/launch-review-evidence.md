## Decisions requiring professional or owner evidence

- Exact legal entity, address and privacy/security contact; actual vendor contracts, regions and retention settings.
- Applicable privacy laws based on users, data, jurisdictions and statutory scope; employee/business-context data must be assessed separately from consumer data.
- Retention schedule, deletion from active storage and backup expiry, legal holds, access/export requests, and a process for verifying requester authority.
- Product-specific taxability, physical/economic nexus, registrations, effective collection dates and remittance responsibilities for the intended U.S. customer footprint. Setting money aside is not evidence that these obligations have been met.
- Independent assessment scope and engagement, manual assistive-technology results, and actual buyer/broker test participants.
