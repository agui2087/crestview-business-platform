## Information we collect

We may collect account and profile information; role and organization information; listing and acquisition-workspace content; buyer/broker messages and transaction status; files a user chooses to upload; billing/customer identifiers and subscription status received from our payment processor; support and pilot feedback; limited first-party usage events; and device, request, security, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.

Do not upload information you are not authorized to share. Acquisition files may contain sensitive commercial, financial, employee, customer, or legal information. Users control what they submit and should minimize personal information wherever possible.

## How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; provide buyer, broker, listing, diligence, document, workforce, and billing workflows; process and secure file uploads; maintain account entitlements; communicate about the service; respond to support and pilot feedback; detect abuse and security incidents; back up and recover the service; improve usability and reliability; and comply with law and enforce our agreements.

## Uploaded acquisition documents

Files are stored in private object storage and associated with authorized users/workspaces. Crestview validates file type/content and screens uploads for security. If managed malware scanning is activated, the file is transmitted to Cloudmersive for automated malware analysis before release. A provider outage or uncertain result causes the upload to fail closed; Crestview does not claim that screening makes a file risk-free.

Users must not use Crestview as the sole system of record for originals. They should maintain an authorized backup of business-critical materials and use qualified professionals to review financial and legal content.

## Service providers and disclosure

We disclose information to service providers only as needed to provide, secure, monitor, bill, support, or recover the service, subject to appropriate contracts. Current/proposed providers and activation status are maintained in the public subprocessor list. This may include hosting (Vercel), authentication/database/object storage (Supabase), payment processing (Stripe), source/automation operations (GitHub), and—only if activated—malware scanning (Cloudmersive) and an approved incident-alert receiver.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights/safety/security, in a corporate transaction subject to appropriate protections, or at a user's direction. User-deleted documents are removed from active metadata and storage; encrypted backup copies may remain inaccessible until the applicable backup expires. Security and billing records may be retained longer where needed for fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations.
